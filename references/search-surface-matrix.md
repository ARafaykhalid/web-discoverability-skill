# Search Surface Matrix

Treat a surface as eligible only when its documented prerequisites and the
project's content type are present. A `RELEVANT` registry cell means evaluate
the requirement; it does not mean the platform will rank, index, or display it.

| Surface | Eligibility | Technical requirements | Content requirements | Structured data requirements | Feed requirements | Crawler requirements | Monitoring | Applicability | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google Search | Public, crawlable, indexable content | status, rendering, canonical, metadata, links | useful intent-satisfying content | only supported truthful features | XML sitemap where useful | Googlebot/resource access | Search Console and HTTP evidence | public search intent | raw HTML, HTTP, GSC when authorized |
| Google snippets | Indexable page and preview-eligible content | title, description, snippet directives | clear visible answers; no clickbait | structured data is not a snippet guarantee | none generally | snippet controls and crawlability | query/page performance | pages seeking previews | HTML, headers, rendered output |
| Google rich results | Supported feature plus page-specific eligibility | crawlability and visible parity | required visible facts | Schema.org validity plus Google eligibility | feature-specific feeds where applicable | resource access | enhancement and rich-result reports | eligible content only | JSON-LD, validator, GSC |
| Google Images | Crawlable image and contextual page | image URLs, dimensions, responsive delivery | descriptive context and alt text | ImageObject only when truthful | image sitemap where useful | Googlebot-Image and resources | image search data when available | meaningful public images | HTML, image HTTP, sitemap |
| Google Videos | Crawlable video on an appropriate page | player, thumbnail, media access | title, transcript/captions, visible placement | VideoObject eligibility | video sitemap where useful | Googlebot-Video and player resources | video search/report data when available | meaningful public video | HTML, JSON-LD, video sitemap |
| Google News | Publisher/editorial content and policy compliance | stable article routes and dates | transparent authorship, corrections, headlines | Article/NewsArticle only when applicable | news sitemap and RSS where useful | Googlebot-News/resources | News/Search Console reports where available | news/editorial sites | article HTML, feed, platform evidence |
| Google Discover | No opt-in guarantee; quality, policy, mobile and image prerequisites | indexability, mobile UX, CWV | original, helpful, non-clickbait, topical content | applicable article/image data only | RSS/news feed may help discovery | normal Google access and preview controls | Discover report when available | editorial/content sites | content, image, GSC |
| Google Lens | Visual content and crawlable image context | image delivery and canonical page | meaningful visual context | image metadata where truthful | image sitemap where useful | image crawler access | image referrals when identifiable | image-rich projects | image HTTP, HTML |
| Google Shopping | Product catalog, policy compliance, and merchant eligibility | crawlable product URLs | accurate product facts | Product/Offer/Review only when genuine | primary/supplemental Merchant feeds | product crawling and no checkout indexing | Merchant Center diagnostics | ecommerce/catalog only | page, feed, MC |
| Google Merchant Center | Verified merchant and accepted feed | feed transport, freshness, identifiers | accurate price, availability, shipping, returns | page/feed parity | primary and supplemental feeds | public product access | diagnostics and disapprovals | ecommerce/catalog only | authorized MC output |
| Google Business Profile | Genuine location/service-area business | consistent public location pages | NAP, hours, services, reviews | LocalBusiness/Organization as applicable | none required | public location access | profile/search performance | local intent only | page facts, authorized profile |
| Google Search Console | Authorized property | DNS/file verification and APIs | report interpretation | enhancement reports | sitemap submission | URL Inspection/crawl stats | exports and reports | property owner only | authorized platform output |
| Bing Search | Public crawlable/indexable content | status, canonical, rendering, links | useful factual content | current Bing support only | sitemap and IndexNow where useful | Bingbot access | Webmaster Tools | public search intent | HTTP, HTML, BWT |

| Bing Copilot | Availability varies by product and region | Bing crawl/index access | concise factual, attributed answers | machine-readable facts where truthful | none guaranteed | Bing/AI crawler policy | Copilot/AI reports only when exposed | public content with evidence | platform output; otherwise unknown |
| Bing AI Performance | Availability varies; never infer access | verified Bing property and reporting | entity/factual consistency | supported markup where documented | none guaranteed | Bing crawl access | AI Performance report where available | authorized property only | platform output; otherwise blocked |
| AI search | Product-specific retrieval and citation behavior | crawlable stable pages and policies | answer blocks, entities, provenance, evidence | structured data and accessible HTML | feeds or llms.txt only when justified | purpose-specific AI crawler policy | identifiable referrals and experiments | public content where useful | page, logs, platform experiment |
| LLM retrieval | Optional downstream retrieval, not a ranking guarantee | stable accessible text and URLs | clear passages, definitions, citations | machine-readable relationships | optional maintained guidance | allow/deny policy by business choice | logs/referrals where measurable | public content and policy choice | HTML, logs, policy |
| Local search | Genuine local or service-area intent | location routes and consistent identity | local services, NAP, hours | LocalBusiness and Organization when truthful | platform profiles | crawler access | profile and organic reports | local businesses only | page, profile, HTTP |
| Social preview | Public shareable URL and fetchable assets | OG/Twitter metadata, image HTTP | truthful title, description, crop | none required | none | preview bot access without private leakage | parser checks and cache monitoring | shareable public pages | raw HTML, image HTTP |

For requirement-level use, inspect each record's `search_surfaces` object. Values
are `RELEVANT`, `NOT_RELEVANT`, or `UNKNOWN`; platform eligibility still requires
current documentation and direct evidence.
