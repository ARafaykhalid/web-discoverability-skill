Plans

Every plan runs the same engine. What changes is how many schedules a workspace may hold and how long the run history is kept, because those are the two costs that scale with use.

Solo

Three schedules, thirty days of run history, one workspace member. Intended for a single analyst who wants a query to arrive on a Monday morning without remembering to run it.

Team

Forty schedules, one year of run history, unlimited members, and per-member delivery preferences. This is the plan most workspaces settle on.

Enterprise

Negotiated schedule limits, seven years of run history, an audit log of every run and every change to a schedule, and a named contact for incidents. Billing is annual and arranged directly.

What is not on this page

No figures are printed here on purpose. This is a benchmark fixture, and a number that no real service charges would be an invented fact sitting in test data where a reader could mistake it for one. The plan names and the shape of the limits are enough to make the page a real document with a real purpose.
