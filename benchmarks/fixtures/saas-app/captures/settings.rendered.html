Workspace settings

Three groups of setting live here: the warehouse connection a schedule runs against, the delivery defaults a new schedule inherits, and the list of members who may see the run history. The forms themselves are filled in after the session is confirmed, which is why the initial response describes them rather than rendering them.

As on the schedules page, the initial HTML deliberately carries the title, the heading, and the bulk of the prose, so the initial and the executed document differ only by the panel below, and the canonical is identical in both. Neither of those is measured on a static-html project, where the JavaScript-rendering requirements are not offered at all. The point is narrower: this fixture is about crawl and index directives disagreeing, and it should not accidentally also be about client-side rendering.

This page moved behind the session guard in the January release recorded on the public release notes. Before that it answered without a session, which is precisely the failure this fixture's boundary exists to make visible.
