Mara Quill

Reporter and critic. Mara has written for The Marram Coast Record since 2019 and covers the harbour board, the ferry contract, and the tidal barrage inquiry. She grew up two streets from the slip and reviews whatever the Harbour Lights puts on, which is a conflict of interest she declares at the top of every review.

Before joining the paper she spent six years on the agricultural press, mostly reporting on drainage boards, which turns out to be the same job with different mud. She is the paper's point of contact for the fishing fleet and for the parish councils at Marram, Ellencote, and Sarn Bach.

Corrections are welcome and are published in full. If a story of hers is wrong, the fastest route is the correction form linked from the story itself; email reaches her more slowly and the desk reads it first.
