Coastal news for the Marram estuary

Three reporters, one photographer, and a print run of four thousand. We cover the harbour board, the ferry contract, the tidal barrage inquiry, and the parish councils that sit between them. Comments are open on every story and are read by a duty editor before they appear.

Harbour dredging pushed back to autumn after survey finds silt has shifted

The dredger booked for June has been released to another port. A bathymetric survey commissioned in April found the silt bank has migrated about forty metres east of where the 2023 plan put it, and the harbour board wants the dredge plan redrawn before the vessel is rebooked. Read the story, or read the 34 reader comments.

Ferry operator opens consultation on a reduced winter timetable

Four sailings a day between November and March, down from seven, with the 07:10 retained for the school run and the 18:40 dropped. The operator says the winter loadings do not cover the crewing; the parish council says the 18:40 is the only way home for anyone working a late shift on the mainland. Read the story.

A play about a tidal barrage should not be this funny. The second act should not be this long. Our review of the estuary drama that half the town has now seen and all of it has an opinion about. Read the review.
