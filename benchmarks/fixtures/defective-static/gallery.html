Three unusable alternatives and one correct one

Every image on this page declares both of its dimensions, so the dimension check is expected to stay silent here and any finding it emits against this page is a false positive. What varies is the alternative text.

The next image is the control. An empty alt is the documented way to mark an image as decorative, and reporting it is the single most common false positive in automated accessibility tooling, so this fixture asserts that the check leaves it alone.

Three findings are expected on this page, one per unusable alternative, and none for the decorative divider.
