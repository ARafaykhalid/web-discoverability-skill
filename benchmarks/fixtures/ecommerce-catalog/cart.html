Your cart

A cart holds one visitor's selection and is different for every visitor, so there is nothing here for a crawler to index. This page therefore serves noindex, and the capture records it as non-indexable. Both halves matter: a noindex on a page the capture calls indexable is exactly the contradiction the crawl checks are looking for, and it would be reported as one if only one half were written.

The exclusion is a meta robots directive rather than a Disallow in robots.txt. A disallowed URL is never fetched, so its noindex is never read, and the two directives would cancel rather than reinforce each other.
