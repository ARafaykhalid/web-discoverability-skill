Checkout

This route exists so the purchase path in this fixture is a real one rather than a word in a file. Together with the cart and the three product pages it is what establishes the ecommerce profile fact, and that fact is what decides whether the product and offer requirements are put in front of an agent at all.

No form fields are shown. A checkout form would carry payment and address inputs, and inventing either in a fixture would make this page look like something it is not. The page is noindex for the same reason the cart is: it holds one order in progress and has no stable content to offer.
